Boston & Portland, ME 5-Day Craft Beer, Seafood & Coastal Itinerary 🍻🦞⚓

Executive Summary & Trip Overview

This itinerary is tailored for a couples' trip from Tuesday, September 1 to Saturday, September 5, 2026, blending historic walking, world-famous New England craft beer, quintessential dive bars, fresh lobster shacks, harbor boat cruises, and coastal hiking.

🎓 Crucial Context: Navigating Boston College Move-In Week (Sept 1)

September 1st is Boston's legendary apartment move-in day ("Allston Christmas"). Traffic around Storrow Drive, Fenway, Allston/Brighton, and Cambridge will be heavily congested.

  • The No-Car Advantage: Staying at Marriott Boston Long Wharf on the harbor completely bypasses student moving gridlock.
  • Zero Rental Car Needed: Your entire journey uses walking, MBTA Subway ("T"), MBTA Harbor Ferries, the scenic Amtrak Downeaster train to Maine, and quick Ubers in Portland.

🏢 Selected Marriott Accommodations

  • 1. Boston (Sept 1–3, 2 Nights): Marriott Boston Long Wharf
    • Situated directly on the harborfront over the Aquarium T Station.
    • Steps from Boston Harbor City Cruises, Faneuil Hall, and North End.
    • Sweeping ocean/city views, outdoor harbor walkway, and top-tier lounge.
  • 2. Portland, ME (Sept 3–5, 2 Nights): AC Hotel Portland Downtown/Waterfront
    • Located on Fore Street right at Ocean Gateway in Old Port.
    • Sleek European-inspired boutique vibe with harbor views.
    • Steps from Casco Bay Ferry pier, top craft beer bars, and seafood shacks.

📍 Day-by-Day Detailed Itinerary

Day 1: Tuesday, Sept 1 – Beacon Hill, Freedom Trail & North End Dives

  • Morning / Check-In: Arrive at Marriott Boston Long Wharf. Drop luggage with bell desk. Grab coffee at Gracenote Coffee or Thinking Cup (Hanover St).
  • Afternoon Walking: Walk through Boston Public Garden & Beacon Hill (Acorn Street), then follow Freedom Trail to historic North End.
  • Lunch: Neptune Oyster (hot buttered lobster roll) or James Hook & Co. (waterfront picnic).
  • Late Afternoon Drink: Cross Charlestown Bridge (or take $3.70 MBTA Ferry) to Warren Tavern (built 1780, historic pub patronized by Paul Revere).
  • Dinner: The Daily Catch (squid ink pasta in hot skillet) or Row 34.
  • Evening Pastry & Dives: Cannolis at Bova's Bakery (24/7 local secret). Dive bar hop at The Tam or Biddy Early's.

Day 2: Wednesday, Sept 2 – Seaport Craft Beer, Harbor Cruise & Row 34

  • Morning Coffee & Walk: Tatte Bakery & Cafe, stroll Boston Harborwalk.
  • Mid-Day Cruise: Walk out hotel door to Boston Harbor Sightseeing Cruise (90 mins), or MBTA ferry to Spectacle Island for a 1-hour coastal trail hike with 360° skyline views.
  • Lunch & Craft Beer: Trillium Brewing Company (Fort Point roof deck) and Harpoon Brewery & Beer Hall (fresh pretzels & beer cheese).
  • Dinner: Row 34 (Fort Point) – Peak raw bar, lobster rolls, and curated craft beer.
  • Nightcap / Dive Bar: Bukowski Tavern (Back Bay) or Sullivan's Tap (North Station).

Day 3: Thursday, Sept 3 – Amtrak Downeaster to Portland & Old Port Dives

  • Morning: Coffee & breakfast at Boston Public Market (George Howell Coffee & Union Square Donuts).
  • 10:00 AM Amtrak Downeaster: 10-min walk/Uber to North Station. Board Amtrak Downeaster to Portland (~2.5 hrs, scenic cafe car with local brews).
  • 12:30 PM Portland Arrival: Arrive at POR, 8-min Uber to AC Hotel Portland Downtown/Waterfront.
  • Lunch: Portland Lobster Co. (pier deck on the water, live music, fresh lobster).
  • Afternoon Walking & Beer: Stroll Old Port cobblestone streets and Harbor Fish Market. Head to East Bayside breweries: Rising Tide Brewing, Austin Street Brewery, and Belleflower Brewing.
  • Dinner: Eventide Oyster Co. (famous brown butter lobster roll) or Highroller Lobster Co.
  • Late Night Dives: Lincoln's (hidden underground $5 cash-only bar) and Rosie's.

Day 4: Friday, Sept 4 – Portland Head Light Coastal Hike & Beer Mecca Pilgrimage

  • Morning Coffee: Tandem Coffee & Bakery (biscuits & pour-over) or Bard Coffee.
  • Morning Coastal Hike & Lighthouse: Uber 10 mins to Fort Williams Park (Cape Elizabeth). Hike cliffside trails around Portland Head Light. Lunch at Bite Into Maine food truck.
  • Afternoon Craft Beer Pilgrimage: Rideshare to Industrial Way: Allagash Brewing Company (Belgian sours & IPAs in outdoor garden), Foundation Brewing, and Definitive Brewing. Or visit Bissell Brothers at Thompson's Point.
  • Dinner: Scales (Maine Wharf) – Pan-fried Haddock, Chowder, and harbor views.
  • Nightcap: Bramhall (underground West End pub) or J's Oyster.

Day 5: Saturday, Sept 5 – Casco Bay Boat Cruise, Chowder & Return

  • Morning Boat Cruise: Casco Bay Lines Mailboat Run (3-hour island ferry cruise) or ferry to Peaks Island for a 4-mile scenic loop walk.
  • Lunch: Gilbert's Chowder House (clam chowder in sourdough bowl) or J's Oyster.
  • Afternoon Return: Take afternoon Amtrak Downeaster back to Boston North Station for departure.
